About Us

Budget-Friendly Hotel
in Vizhinjam

Jumayira Residency is a small hotel in Vizhinjam, about 1.5 km from Kovalam Beach. We've been running since 2016 — nothing fancy, just clean rooms and people who'll help you out when you need it.

Close to the harbour, the beach, and the main road. Good for families, couples, or anyone visiting Kovalam on a budget.
