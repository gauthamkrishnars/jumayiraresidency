Welcome

Jumayira International is a welcoming 3-star hotel nestled in the heart of Kovalam, Kerala. With comfortable AC and Non-AC rooms, we offer the perfect base for exploring the stunning beaches and backwaters of God's Own Country.

Whether you're here for a relaxing beach holiday, a spiritual retreat to the nearby temples, or a family vacation, our warm and attentive staff ensures your stay is nothing short of memorable. We look forward to welcoming you.
